FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You download the file, double-click it, paste your Google key once, and you're ready. Everything is done with the mouse.

What is the Google API key?

It's a personal code Google gives you for free to search Google Maps. Creating it takes about ten minutes, and the app shows you where to get it.

How much does Google cost?

Google offers a free quota every month, enough for regular local prospecting. Beyond it, searches are billed by Google to your account. You can set a budget alert so you're never surprised.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere Google Maps lists businesses: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia and more. Just type the city the way you would on Google Maps (for example “Austin TX” or “Leeds UK”).

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. BridgeToLeads runs on Windows 10 / 11 and on Mac, with one version for Apple silicon Macs (M1 to M4) and one for Intel Macs.

Windows or my Mac shows a warning on first launch. Is that normal?

Yes, for recent software they don't know yet. On Windows, click “More info” then “Run anyway”. On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. You only do it once.

How does the license work?

Right after payment, your license key appears on the thank-you page; it is valid on 2 computers. You paste it on first launch, and that's it. It is checked online once a day (only the key and an anonymous computer ID are sent); without Internet, the software stays usable for 14 days. To change computers, “License” button → “Release”. If the purchase is refunded, the license is deactivated.

Am I allowed to contact these businesses?

Contacting businesses about their professional activity is generally allowed, as long as your offer relates to their business and you let them opt out of further contact; rules vary by country (CAN-SPAM and TCPA in the US, CASL in Canada, PECR in the UK…). If in doubt, check with a legal adviser.
