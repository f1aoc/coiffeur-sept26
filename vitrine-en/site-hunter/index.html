FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You download the file, double-click it, and the app opens in your browser. Everything is done with the mouse.

Is the software in English?

Yes. The app, the PDF reports, the exports and the messages are in English (or in French: you choose in Settings). By default, Site Hunter uses your computer's language, and in English the Google search is not limited to one country.

Is the Google key required?

Only for the “trade + city” search. You can also import your own lists (CSV or Excel, with at least the name and the website address): in that case, no key is needed.

How much does using Google cost?

Google offers a free quota every month. Before each search, Site Hunter shows the maximum number of requests and an estimated cost, so you are never surprised.

How can I be sure a site is really down?

Each site is tested twice before being declared down, then opened in a real browser. Every problem comes with its evidence (error message, expiry date, screenshot). If the analysis could not see a site, it is marked “To recheck”, never wrongly classified.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. Site Hunter works on Windows 10 / 11 and on Mac, with one version for Apple silicon Macs (M1 to M4) and one for Intel Macs.

Windows or my Mac shows a warning on first launch. Is that normal?

Yes, for recent software they don't know yet. On Windows, click “More info” then “Run anyway”. On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. You only do it once.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer, and nowhere else. Your prospects, your notes and your keys are not sent to any server; the keys are encrypted. Only your license key is checked online, once a day.

How does the license work?

Right after payment, your license key appears on the thank-you page; it is valid on 2 computers. You paste it on first launch, and that's it. It is checked online once a day; without Internet, the software stays usable for 14 days. To change computers, release the license in one click. If the purchase is refunded, the license is deactivated.

Am I allowed to contact these businesses?

Contacting businesses about their professional activity is generally allowed, as long as you offer a simple way to refuse further contact; rules vary by country (GDPR in Europe, CAN-SPAM in the US, PECR in the UK…). Site Hunter helps: built-in opt-out list, one-click prospect deletion, automatic deletion of prospects never contacted after 12 months. If in doubt, check with a legal adviser.
