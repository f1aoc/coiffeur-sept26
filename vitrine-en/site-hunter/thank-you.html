Getting started

Ready in 10 minutes

Launch the app

On Windows

Double-click ChasseurDeSites.exe in your Downloads folder.
If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway. Only once.

On Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded .zip file to unzip it.
  2. Drag ChasseurDeSites into your Applications folder, then open it.
If your Mac refuses to open it the first time, click OK, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.

A small “Site Hunter” window opens. The first time, it downloads the analysis browser (about 150 MB, a few minutes), then the app opens by itself in your web browser.

Keep the small window open (you can minimise it): closing it stops the app.

Activate your license, then set up your agency

The app asks for your license key: the one shown higher up on this page (it starts with “CDS-”). Paste it, then click Activate (Internet connection required). It is valid on 2 computers.

Then, in Settings → Your agency, enter your name, logo, colour and contact details. They will appear on your PDF reports and in your messages. The language (English or French) can be changed in Settings → Language.

Find businesses

Without a Google key: in New analysis, drop a CSV or Excel file with at least the name and the website address of each business.

With the “trade + city” search: you need a Google Maps key (free to create).

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create a new project (for example “prospecting”).
  3. In Billing, add a bank card. Google requires it even for the free quota.
  4. In APIs & Services → Library, enable “Places API (New)” (with “New”).
  5. In APIs & Services → Credentials, click Create credentials → API key and copy the key (it starts with “AIza”).
  6. Paste it in Settings → API keys in Site Hunter.
Tip: in Billing → Budgets & alerts, create an alert (for example $10 a month). Site Hunter also shows an estimated cost before each search.

Run your first analysis

In Search, type a trade (for example plumber) and your cities, click Search, then Start the analysis. Follow the progress, then open the Results: the worst sites are at the top.

Click a business name to see its screenshots, create its PDF report and copy the email, call script or text message already written.

Tick “Priority prospects” to keep only broken or outdated sites of well-rated businesses on Google: these are your best calls.
