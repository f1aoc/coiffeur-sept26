Getting started

Ready in 10 minutes

Launch the app

On Windows

  1. Right-click AuditExpress-Windows.zip (Downloads folder) → Extract All… → Extract.
  2. In the extracted folder, open AuditExpress and double-click AuditExpress.exe.
If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway. Only once.

On Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded .zip file to unzip it.
  2. Drag AuditExpress into your Applications folder, then open it.
If your Mac refuses to open it the first time, click OK, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.

The first time, Audit Express downloads the browser used for screenshots (about 150 MB, a few minutes), then opens by itself in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765.

On Windows, a black window stays open: keep it (you can minimise it); closing it stops Audit Express.

Activate your license, then set up your agency

Audit Express asks for your license key: the one shown higher up on this page (it starts with “AEX-”). Paste it, then click Activate (Internet connection required). It is valid on 2 computers.

Then, in Settings → White label, enter your agency name, logo, colours, contact details and booking link. They will appear on your PDF reports and in your emails. The language (English or French) can be changed in Settings → Language.

Add your Google PageSpeed key (free, recommended)

Speed measurements come from Google PageSpeed. Without a key, Google limits the number of measurements and speed is often marked “not checked”. The key is free:

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create a new project (for example “audits”).
  3. In APIs & Services → Library, enable “PageSpeed Insights API”.
  4. In APIs & Services → Credentials, click Create credentials → API key and copy the key (it starts with “AIza”).
  5. Paste it in Settings → Google PageSpeed API key in Audit Express, then Save the key.
The key is stored encrypted on your computer: it never appears in reports or exports.

Run your first audit

In New audit, type a website address (and, if you like, the business name and city), then click Audit. Less than a minute later, you see the grade, the problems found and the report preview.

Untick a problem to remove it from the report, add your comment, then click Download the PDF. The covering email is ready just below: Copy the email.

To audit a whole list (CSV or Excel), use Batch audit: every report arrives in a single ZIP file.
