FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You download the software, double-click it, and it opens in your browser. Everything is done with the mouse.

Is the software in English?

Yes. The interface, the PDF reports and the emails are in English (or in French: you choose in Settings). By default, Audit Express uses your computer's language.

What exactly does the audit check?

37 checks in 7 categories: speed, display on phones, visibility on Google, security and technical, ease of contact, legal compliance (business information, privacy policy, cookie consent) and accessibility. Every problem comes with its evidence.

What if a check cannot be done?

It is marked “not checked” and does not count in the score: Audit Express never makes up a result. You can also remove any point from the report in one click before sending it.

Is the Google key required?

No. It is free and recommended for speed measurements (Google PageSpeed): without it, Google limits the number of measurements. It takes a few minutes to create; the guide is on the thank-you page.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. Audit Express works on Windows 10 / 11 and on Mac, with one version for Apple silicon Macs (M1 to M4) and one for Intel Macs.

Windows or my Mac shows a warning on first launch. Is that normal?

Yes, for recent software they don't know yet. On Windows, click “More info” then “Run anyway”. On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. You only do it once.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer, and nowhere else: audits, reports, logo and settings. Only the address of the audited site is sent to Google PageSpeed to measure its speed, and your license key is checked online once a day.

How does the license work?

Right after payment, your license key appears on the thank-you page; it is valid on 2 computers. You paste it on first launch, and that's it. It is checked online once a day; without Internet, the software stays usable for 14 days. To change computers, release the license in one click. If the purchase is refunded, the license is deactivated.

Can I audit anyone's website?

Audit Express visits a site like any visitor: it only reads public pages, respects the site's robots.txt file and pauses between pages so as not to overload it. The report is for information only and is neither a security audit nor legal advice.
