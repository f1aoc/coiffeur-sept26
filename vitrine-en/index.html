FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which app should I choose?

You build websites for businesses that don't have one? Start with BridgeToLeads. You offer redesigns or maintenance? Site Hunter is made for you. You want to convince faster with a clear report? Audit Express. The bundle of all three ($199) covers the whole chain, from finding the prospect to signing the deal.

Is there a subscription?

No. Each app is paid once, updates included. Only Google Maps searches (and Audit Express speed tests) go through your own Google key, which comes with a free quota every month. After purchase, you get a license key valid on 2 computers.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. All three apps run on Windows 10 / 11 and on Mac (Apple silicon and Intel).

Am I allowed to contact these businesses?

Contacting businesses about their professional activity is generally allowed, as long as your offer relates to their business and they can opt out of further contact; rules vary by country (CAN-SPAM and TCPA in the US, CASL in Canada, PECR in the UK…). Our apps only use public business information. If in doubt, check with a legal adviser.
