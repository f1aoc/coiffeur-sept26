Cookie Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

1. In short

The pages of vorelik.com set no cookies and store nothing in your browser. There is no audience measurement, no advertising, no social media button and no embedded video. Fonts are hosted on our own site: displaying a page contacts no other service.

2. On vorelik.com

Cookies
none
Browser storage
none (no « localStorage » or « sessionStorage »)
Audience measurement
none
Advertising, social media, embedded videos
none
Fonts
hosted on vorelik.com (no request to Google Fonts)

Like any website, our host technically receives each visitor's IP address and browser in order to display the pages (see the privacy policy). This is not a cookie and nothing is stored on your device.

3. Payment, licence and download

  • Payment: the « Buy » button takes you to Stripe's payment page (buy.stripe.com). This page, not hosted by VORELIK, sets its own cookies, needed for payment and fraud prevention. See Stripe's cookie policy.
  • « Thank you » page: after payment, it requests your licence key from our licence server (Cloudflare) using the payment number in the page address. No cookie is set.
  • Download: the software is downloaded from our storage space at Cloudflare. A simple file download sets no cookie on our side.

4. Why there is no consent banner

A consent banner is only required when a site sets cookies that are not strictly necessary (audience measurement, advertising, social media…). vorelik.com sets none, so there is nothing to accept or refuse. If this ever changes, this page will be updated and your consent will be asked before anything is set.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, delete or block the cookies stored by the sites you visit at any time from your browser settings: Chrome (Settings → Privacy and security), Firefox (Settings → Privacy & Security), Safari (Settings → Privacy), Edge (Settings → Cookies and site permissions).
