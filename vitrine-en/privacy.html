1. Data controller

VORELIK (Patrick Tabountchikoff, sole trader), 61 rue de Lyon, 75012 Paris, France, publisher of Audit Express. Contact for any question about your data: support@vorelik.com.

We collect only what is needed to sell licences, make them work and answer support requests. We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not send advertising. This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Purchase data

  • Data: name, email address, billing address if requested, product purchased, amount, date. Card details are entered at Stripe and are never sent to us.
  • Why: to perform the sale (legal basis: contract), keep the accounts (legal obligation), and handle refunds and payment disputes.

3. License data

  • Data: the license key, the product, and an identifier of each activated computer (a 16-character fingerprint computed from the computer, which contains neither your name nor your files). At each check, our server also receives the connection IP address, like any web server.
  • Why: to activate the license, limit its use to 2 computers and deactivate it in case of a refund or payment dispute (legal basis: contract).
  • Where: the key and computer identifiers are stored with the payment, at Stripe. The license server (Cloudflare) does not keep anything itself.

4. What stays on your computer

The websites you audit, the reports and your settings are stored only on your computer. They are not sent to us. Your API keys (for example Google PageSpeed) also stay on your computer, and requests go directly from your computer to the service concerned, under its own terms.

For this data, you are the data controller: you are responsible for using it in accordance with the data protection and direct marketing rules that apply to you.

5. Website and cookies

The pages of vorelik.com set no cookies and use no audience measurement or advertising tools. Fonts are hosted on our own site: displaying a page contacts no other service. The payment page, hosted by Stripe, uses its own cookies, needed for payment and fraud prevention. Details: cookie policy.

7. Retention periods

  • Sales data and accounting records: 10 years (French Commercial Code, article L123-22).
  • License key and computer identifiers: as long as the license exists; a released computer is removed from the list.
  • Support emails: 3 years after the last exchange.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, restrict its processing or object to it, by writing to support@vorelik.com from the address used for the purchase. We reply within one month. Data the law requires us to keep (accounting) cannot be deleted before the end of its retention period.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the French data protection authority (CNIL, www.cnil.fr/en) or to the authority of your country.
